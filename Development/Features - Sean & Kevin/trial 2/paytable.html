Golden Grid Paytable

All awards start from 3 matching reels from the left. The symbol award doubles on reel 4 and doubles again on reel 5 before the total number of ways is applied.

Core rules

How awards work

  • Multiway awards pay on consecutive reels from reel 1.
  • 3 matches unlock the base symbol award.
  • 4 matches pay 2x the base award per way.
  • 5 matches pay 4x the base award per way.
  • Low-award symbols appear 6 times more often on every reel strip.
  • High-award symbols appear once per reel strip and start above 1000.
